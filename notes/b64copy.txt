Convert to base 64 and copy:

$p = "C:\Users\shash\Documents\My Files\RISC-M\Old\FPGA-Based-LIDAR-ISP\hps\lidar_script.c"
$b64 = [Convert]::ToBase64String([IO.File]::ReadAllBytes($p))
([regex]::Matches($b64, '.{1,64}').Value -join "`r`n") | Set-Clipboard

In PuTTY, run:
cat > /tmp/lidar_script.b64
 
then paste it and press ctrl+D

then decode the file:
base64 -d /tmp/lidar_script.b64 > /root/lidar_script.c

and compile:
gcc -std=gnu11 -Wall -Wextra -O2 lidar_script.c -o lidar_script
